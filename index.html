Wear a little holiday magic

Red and white are the stars. Mix and match, add a festive accessory, or keep it simple with a polished casual look.

Think red tops, white shirts, knitwear, skirts, trousers, dresses, clean sneakers, loafers, or other comfortable smart-casual pieces.

Optional: subtle gold accessories, Christmas headbands, ribbons, or a touch of sparkle. Please wear something comfortable for games, photos, dinner, and bowling.

Mystery Gift Auction

Bring a wrapped surprise and get ready to bid, bluff, and laugh!

Exchange gift budget₱500per gift

How it works

Everyone brings one wrapped gift without revealing what's inside. Leads will give each attendee play money, then gifts will be auctioned one by one using funny descriptions or hints. Participants bid without knowing the exact item — so choose your strategy wisely!
